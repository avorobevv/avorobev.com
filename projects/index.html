  1. 01 Healthcare · 2025

    Claims360

    Real-time revenue cycle analytics on an Azure Databricks lakehouse.

  2. 02 Smart cities · 2025

    UrbanPulse

    Real-time city traffic intelligence from streaming IoT sensors.

    A Lakeflow pipeline that ingests traffic sensor events, cleans and deduplicates them, and rolls them up into per-minute and per-hour zone facts. Databricks SQL dashboards surface congestion hotspots, active alerts and sensor health so operations teams can act before bottlenecks cascade.

    Scale
    20 sensors · 5 zones
