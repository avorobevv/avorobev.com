Hello, world

Why this site exists, and what I plan to write about here.

Most of what I learn at work ends up in a Slack thread, a client deck, or a notebook nobody opens again. This is a place to write some of it down properly, as well as more general thoughts about where things are going.

What to expect

  • Data engineering in practice: Databricks, lakehouse design, and the unglamorous parts of getting pipelines to production.
  • Agentic engineering: what actually works when you wire agents, MCP servers, and data platforms together.
  • Design: how data products should look and feel, and why that matters.
  • Economy and trends: why I think certain things are how they are, and different trends I've been seeing at work and in life.

Most posts are intended to be short and practical; some may be longer thought pieces. If something here is useful (or wrong), I'd love to hear about it on LinkedIn.
